ENGINE 1 · SHAPEProcrustes least squares lines your signature up with your template (rotation by OLS). SVD-TLS then measures what is left over, outside your natural variation.
ENGINE 2 · EDGESSobel edge detection on the last video frame: G = √(Gx² + Gy²). Edge clouds are compared with chamfer distance.
ENGINE 3 · SPEEDCubic spline x(t), y(t), then the 1st derivative. Speed at every point along the path, plus total time.
ENGINE 4 · ACCELERATION2nd derivative of the same spline, compared point by point along the path.
DECISIONShape + edges form the FORM group; speed + acceleration form the MOTION group. Both must pass on their own, so a confident hand can never make up for a wrong shape.
QUALITY · SMOOTHNESS3rd derivative (jerk) gives a smoothness value. It keeps bad samples out of your enrollment. Rootfinding marks where the pen slowed down.
